Installation complete

1. Reopen Autodesk Fusion.
2. Open a design, switch to Solid, and open Create > Printable Threads.
3. If the menu is missing, press Shift+S, choose the Add-Ins tab, select
   PrintableThreads, click Run, and enable Run on Startup.

The tool dialog should show version 0.5.2.

Windows: uninstall through Settings > Apps > Installed apps.
Mac: close Fusion and remove PrintableThreads from your Fusion API/AddIns folder.
Saved settings and installation backups are retained when uninstalling.
